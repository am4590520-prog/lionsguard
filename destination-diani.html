Diani Beach

Relax on Kenya’s beautiful white sand beaches.

Explore Diani Beach

Diani Beach is one of Africa’s most famous coastal destinations, known for its crystal-clear waters, white sandy beaches and tropical palm trees. Located along Kenya’s south coast, Diani offers the perfect combination of relaxation, adventure and luxury.

Visitors can enjoy beach holidays, water sports, marine excursions and breathtaking sunsets along the Indian Ocean. Diani is the perfect place to relax after a wildlife safari adventure.

Beach Activities

Swimming & Relaxation

Enjoy peaceful moments along the white sandy beaches while swimming in the warm turquoise waters of the Indian Ocean.

Water Sports

Experience exciting activities such as snorkeling, scuba diving, kite surfing, jet skiing and deep-sea fishing.

Marine Excursions

Explore nearby coral reefs, dolphin tours and Wasini Island trips while discovering the beauty of Kenya’s coastal marine life.
