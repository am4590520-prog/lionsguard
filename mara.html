About Maasai Mara

The Maasai Mara National Reserve is Kenya’s most iconic safari destination, known worldwide for its incredible wildlife and vast open plains. It forms part of the Serengeti ecosystem and offers one of the best wildlife viewing experiences in Africa.

Visitors can witness the famous Great Migration, where millions of wildebeest and zebras cross into Kenya from Tanzania. The reserve is also home to the Big Five — lions, elephants, leopards, rhinos, and buffalo — making every game drive unforgettable.

Highlights

  • Big Five wildlife viewing
  • The Great Migration (seasonal)
  • Hot air balloon safaris
  • Maasai cultural experiences
