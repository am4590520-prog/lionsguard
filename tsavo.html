Tsavo National Park

Kenya’s Wildest and Largest Safari Destination

About Tsavo

Tsavo National Park is Kenya’s largest wildlife conservation area, divided into Tsavo East and Tsavo West. It is famous for its vast landscapes and iconic red elephants.

The park features diverse scenery including lava flows, hills, rivers, and natural springs such as Mzima Springs, making it one of the most unique safari destinations in Kenya.

Highlights

  • Famous red elephants
  • Vast untouched wilderness
  • Mzima Springs crystal-clear waters
  • Diverse landscapes and wildlife
