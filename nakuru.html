Lake Nakuru National Park

Flamingos, Rhinos & Scenic Beauty

About Lake Nakuru

Lake Nakuru National Park is one of Kenya’s most famous parks, known for its stunning flamingo populations that form a pink shoreline along the lake.

The park is also a sanctuary for endangered black and white rhinos, making it one of the best places in Kenya to see these rare animals up close.

Highlights

  • Flamingo-lined lake shores
  • Rhino sanctuary
  • Scenic viewpoints and waterfalls
  • Birdwatching paradise
