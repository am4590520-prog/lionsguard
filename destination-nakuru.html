Lake Nakuru

Famous for flamingos and rhinos.

Explore Lake Nakuru National Park

Lake Nakuru National Park is one of Kenya’s most beautiful wildlife parks, located in the Great Rift Valley. The park is internationally famous for its flamingos, white rhinos, black rhinos and incredible birdlife.

Visitors can enjoy scenic landscapes, acacia forests, waterfalls and unforgettable wildlife sightings including lions, giraffes, buffaloes, zebras and leopards. The park is also one of the best places in Kenya to see rhinos in their natural habitat.

Safari Experience

Morning Game Drive

Start your safari with an exciting game drive around Lake Nakuru. Spot flamingos along the lake shores, rhinos grazing in the park and other wildlife roaming freely across the savannah.

Explore Scenic Attractions

Visit beautiful viewpoints such as Baboon Cliff and enjoy panoramic views of the lake and surrounding landscapes. Discover waterfalls, forests and diverse bird species throughout the park.

Photography & Relaxation

Capture unforgettable safari moments and enjoy the peaceful atmosphere of one of Kenya’s top wildlife destinations before returning to Nairobi or continuing your safari adventure.
