Amboseli Safari

Elephants with breathtaking Mount Kilimanjaro views.

About Amboseli National Park

Amboseli National Park is one of Kenya’s most famous safari destinations, known for its large elephant herds and stunning views of Mount Kilimanjaro. The park offers incredible wildlife experiences with lions, cheetahs, giraffes, zebras, buffaloes and hundreds of bird species.

This safari gives you the perfect combination of adventure, wildlife, photography and relaxation while enjoying the beauty of Kenya’s natural landscapes.

Safari Itinerary

Day 1 – Nairobi to Amboseli

Depart from Nairobi in the morning and drive through scenic landscapes towards Amboseli National Park. Arrive in time for lunch at the lodge followed by an afternoon game drive where you may spot elephants, lions, zebras and wildebeests.

Day 2 – Full Day Game Drive

Spend the day exploring Amboseli with morning and afternoon game drives. Enjoy spectacular views of Mount Kilimanjaro while observing wildlife around the swamps and open plains. Capture unforgettable safari photos and experience the beauty of African wildlife up close.

Day 3 – Return to Nairobi

Enjoy an early morning game drive before breakfast. Afterwards depart Amboseli and travel back to Nairobi with amazing memories of your safari adventure.
