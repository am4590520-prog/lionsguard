Samburu National Reserve

Discover the unique wildlife and beauty of northern Kenya.

Explore Samburu

Samburu National Reserve is one of Kenya’s most unique safari destinations, located in the dry northern region of the country. The reserve is famous for its rare wildlife species known as the “Samburu Special Five” which include the Grevy’s zebra, reticulated giraffe, Somali ostrich, gerenuk and Beisa oryx.

The Ewaso Nyiro River flows through the reserve attracting elephants, lions, leopards and many other wildlife species. Samburu offers visitors a true wilderness experience with breathtaking landscapes, rich culture and unforgettable game drives.
