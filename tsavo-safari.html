Tsavo Adventure

Explore Kenya’s largest wilderness park.

About Tsavo National Park

Tsavo National Park is Kenya’s largest and one of the oldest wildlife parks, divided into Tsavo East and Tsavo West. Famous for its red elephants, dramatic landscapes, lava flows, rivers and diverse wildlife, Tsavo offers an unforgettable safari experience in the heart of the wild.

Visitors can enjoy breathtaking scenery, exciting game drives and the chance to spot lions, elephants, leopards, buffaloes, giraffes, zebras and many other animal species across the vast wilderness.

Safari Itinerary

Day 1 – Nairobi to Tsavo

Depart from Nairobi in the morning and drive towards Tsavo National Park. Arrive in time for lunch at your safari lodge before heading for an afternoon game drive where you may encounter elephants, lions and other wildlife in their natural habitat.

Day 2 – Full Day Safari Experience

Spend the day exploring the beauty of Tsavo with morning and afternoon game drives. Visit scenic attractions such as Mzima Springs, volcanic landscapes and open savannah plains while enjoying amazing wildlife photography opportunities.

Day 3 – Return to Nairobi

Enjoy an early morning game drive before breakfast and later depart Tsavo for Nairobi with unforgettable safari memories and experiences.
