About Amboseli

Amboseli National Park is one of Kenya’s most scenic safari destinations, famous for its large elephant herds and breathtaking views of Mount Kilimanjaro, Africa’s highest peak.

The park offers open plains, swamps, and acacia woodlands, creating a diverse ecosystem that supports a wide range of wildlife including lions, cheetahs, giraffes, zebras, and over 400 bird species.

Highlights

  • Large elephant herds
  • Views of Mount Kilimanjaro
  • Excellent wildlife photography opportunities
  • Rich birdlife and wetlands
