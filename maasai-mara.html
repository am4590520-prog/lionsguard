3 Days Maasai Mara Safari

Experience the Big Five and the Great Migration in Kenya’s most famous reserve with expert guides, comfortable accommodations, and unforgettable game drives.

Safari Overview

The Maasai Mara National Reserve is world-famous for its breathtaking savannahs, abundant wildlife, and the dramatic annual migration of wildebeest and zebras. This three-day safari allows you to witness lions, elephants, leopards, rhinos, and buffalos in their natural habitat while staying in luxurious camps.

Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival & Evening Game Drive

Arrive at the Maasai Mara airstrip or road transfer from Nairobi. Check into your lodge or luxury tented camp. After freshening up, embark on an afternoon game drive to catch glimpses of grazing wildlife, including elephants, zebras, and gazelles. Return to the camp for a delicious dinner under the stars.

Day 2: Full-Day Game Drive & Maasai Village Visit

Start the day early with a sunrise game drive. Spot predators like lions and leopards as they hunt. Enjoy a picnic lunch in the heart of the reserve. In the afternoon, visit a nearby Maasai village to learn about traditional culture, dances, and beadwork. Return to camp for dinner and relaxation.

Day 3: Early Morning Drive & Departure

Wake up for an early morning game drive to catch the wildlife at its most active time. After the drive, return to camp for breakfast and check-out. Transfer back to Nairobi or your next destination with memories of the unforgettable Maasai Mara experience.
